Cadence 0.6.0-rc.3 against eight engines on the CCRL Blitz list
Played 2026-11-11 and 2026-11-12, from 10:22 to 14:15 UTC the next day.

THE RESULT

4,000 games, 500 against each of eight engines. Ordo, with each engine's
rating fixed at its CCRL Blitz rating, gives Cadence:

  seven engines, Reckless 0.4.0 left out   2796.4, +/- 9.4 at 95% (sigma 4.8)
  all eight engines                         2806.1, +/- 8.8 at 95% (sigma 4.5)

The headline is the first. Reckless 0.4.0 was played and left out under a
rule set before the games: the ratings the engines imply for Cadence may
span at most 80. With Reckless they span 121, from 2758 to 2879, and
without it 64, from 2758 to 2822. Reckless's own 95% interval, 2854 to
2903, overlaps none of the other seven's (analysis-eight.txt,
analysis-seven.txt).

This is an estimate calibrated against CCRL ratings. It is not a CCRL
rating.

THE HOST

An Apple M5 Max running macOS, with six performance cores: six games at
once, one for each, on mains power, kept awake by caffeinate, with nothing
else running. The host's thermal and performance warning levels were read
every minute; none of the 1,672 readings showed a warning.

THE ENGINES' SETTINGS

One search thread (Threads=1 where an engine has the option), 256 MB of
hash each, pondering off. The Syzygy tables up to five pieces for the
engines that take a path: Cadence, Halogen 8 and Supernova 2.4. Every other
option at its default.

Cadence is the published 0.6.0-rc.3 release's macOS arm64 binary, its
SHA-256 as the release's SHA256SUMS lists it (anchors.csv).

THE TIME CONTROL

CCRL Blitz plays 2 minutes plus 1 second a game on its reference machine,
an Intel i7-4770K, where Stockfish 10's default bench takes 2,054 ms, the
figure CCRL's testers published on TalkChess in January 2022.

Here the same bench, Stockfish 10 built from its sf_10 tag for arm64, ran as
six copies at once, the games' concurrency: one warm-up round and five
timed. T is the median of the 30 timed copies: 861 ms, read on 2026-11-10
and again just before the games. So f = 861 / 2054 = 0.419, and every game
was played at 120 s x 0.419 plus 1 s x 0.419 a move: 50.28 s plus
0.419 s. T read 865 ms after the games, 0.46% more.

THE BOOK

book.pgn: 250 openings of eight moves, drawn with seed 600 from
8moves_v3.pgn in the official-stockfish/books repository at commit
a804a97e709f40aa75a00f9d19e84915b7a3a45c, CC0-1.0. Each opening was played
with both colours against every engine, in order.

THE ENGINES

anchors.csv lists each engine's repository, the tag and commit it was
built from, the toolchain and build command, and the binary's SHA-256.
Each was built from source for arm64, never run through Rosetta. Two
source changes, in patches/:
  clovis-III.diff    deletes an unused include of an x86-only header,
                     which clang refuses on arm64
  stockfish-10.diff  names arm64 on the Makefile's Darwin -arch line

The ratings are from the CCRL Blitz list computed 2026-10-17, read
2026-10-24.

How the eight were chosen: a locating probe first, outside the fit
(probe.pgn), 100 games each against Tantabus 2.0.0, Velvet 1.2.0, Clovis
III and Reckless 0.4.0, which put Cadence near 2801. Then eight engines
rated within 150 of that, four above and four below, each a different
project. Each played a 10-game smoke test (smoke-tests.pgn). Donna 4.1 lost
3 of its 10 on time and was replaced by Blunder 8.5.5.

THE MATCH

fastchess 1.8.2, as a gauntlet: -rounds 250 -games 2 -repeat
-srand 20261110, concurrency 6, no adjudication. Every decisive game ended
in mate, and every draw by repetition, the fifty-move rule, insufficient
material or stalemate.

games.pgn is the PGN fastchess wrote, with one change: the four accented
letters in some Opening tags are spelled without their accents, because
this repository keeps its text ASCII. The moves, results, clocks and every
other tag are as written.

STABILITY

No crash, disconnect or illegal move from any engine, and no game lost on
time by either side. No move overran its clock budget by more than 0.5 s.
Every game's termination was normal.

THE FIT

With Ordo 1.2.6, from the root of this repository:

  ordo -Q -D -s 1000 -F 95 \
    -m docs/calibration/gauntlet-0.6.0-rc.3/ordo-anchors-seven.csv \
    -p docs/calibration/gauntlet-0.6.0-rc.3/games.pgn

-F 68.27 gives sigma in place of the 95% margin, and ordo-anchors-eight.csv
the fit with Reckless 0.4.0. The interval can move a little between runs,
because Ordo estimates it by simulation. The outputs are ordo-seven-95.txt,
ordo-seven-sigma.txt, ordo-eight-95.txt and ordo-eight-sigma.txt.

analysis-eight.txt and analysis-seven.txt give each engine's implied rating
for Cadence, its residual against the fit, and the spread. per-anchor.tsv
gives Cadence's wins, draws and losses against each engine, by colour, and
how the games ended.
